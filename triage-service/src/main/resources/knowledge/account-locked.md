# Locked out after too many sign-in attempts

After 10 failed sign-in attempts, Noteflow locks the account for 30 minutes to protect it. The lock lifts automatically; you do not need to contact support.

To get back in sooner, reset your password with **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. A successful reset unlocks the account immediately.

If you did not try to sign in yourself, reset your password and turn on two-factor authentication.
