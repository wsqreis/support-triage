# Notes not syncing between devices

Check these in order:

1. **Connection**: the sync icon in the sidebar shows a cloud. A crossed-out cloud means the device is offline.
2. **Same account**: confirm both devices are signed in with the same email in **Settings → Account**.
3. **Update the app**: sync needs Noteflow 4.2 or newer on every device.
4. **Device limit**: the Free plan syncs 2 devices. A third device signs in but does not sync.
5. **Force a sync**: on desktop press Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac); on mobile, pull down on the note list.

If a note still does not appear after these steps, do not delete or reinstall the app: unsynced notes exist only on that device. Contact support and include the app version from **Settings → About**.
