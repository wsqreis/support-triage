# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Turn on 2FA in **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**. Noteflow supports authenticator apps (TOTP) and gives you 10 one-time backup codes when you enable it. Store the backup codes somewhere safe.

Lost your phone or authenticator app? Sign in with one of your backup codes, then disable and re-enable 2FA to set up the new device.

If you have no backup codes left, contact support from the email address on the account. For your security, recovering an account without backup codes requires identity verification and takes up to 3 business days.
