# Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address of your Noteflow account. We send a reset link within a few minutes.
3. The link is valid for 1 hour. Open it on any device and choose a new password of at least 10 characters.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address you signed up with. Reset links can be requested at most 5 times per hour.

Signed up with Google or Apple? You have no Noteflow password: use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** instead.
