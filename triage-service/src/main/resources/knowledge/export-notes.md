# Exporting your notes

- **A single note**: open it, then **⋯ → Export** and choose Markdown or PDF. Available on all plans.
- **All notes at once** (Pro): **Settings → Data → Export all**. You receive an email with a ZIP of Markdown files, organised by notebook, usually within 30 minutes. The link is valid for 7 days.

Attachments are included in the ZIP in an `attachments` folder. Exports never delete or change your notes.
