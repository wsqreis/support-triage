# Refunds

- **Annual plans** can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge.
- **Monthly plans** are not refunded, but you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges** are always refunded. Contact support with the date and amount; once confirmed, the refund reaches your card within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play must be refunded by Apple or Google, following their own policies.
