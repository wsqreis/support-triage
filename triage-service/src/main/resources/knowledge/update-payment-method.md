# Updating your payment method and invoices

Change your card in **Settings → Plan → Payment method**. The new card is used from the next charge.

Invoices for every payment are in **Settings → Plan → Invoices**, as PDF. To add a company name or tax ID to future invoices, fill in **Billing details** on the same page; past invoices cannot be changed.

If a payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email you each time. Your account stays on Pro during that period.
