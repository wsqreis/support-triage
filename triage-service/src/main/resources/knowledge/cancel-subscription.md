# Cancelling or downgrading Pro

Go to **Settings → Plan → Cancel Pro**. You keep Pro until the end of the current billing period, then the account moves to Free. Your notes are never deleted when you downgrade.

On Free, sync is limited to 2 devices: you will be asked to choose which ones stay connected.

Subscribed through the App Store or Google Play? Cancel in your Apple or Google account settings; Noteflow cannot cancel those subscriptions for you.
