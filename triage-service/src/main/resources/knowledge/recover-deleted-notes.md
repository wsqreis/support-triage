# Recovering deleted notes

Deleted notes go to **Trash** and stay there for 30 days on every plan. Open Trash in the sidebar, select the note and choose **Restore**.

After 30 days notes are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by support.

**Earlier versions of a note** (Pro): open the note, then **⋯ → Version history** to restore any version from the last 90 days. This also helps when text was deleted by mistake inside a note.
