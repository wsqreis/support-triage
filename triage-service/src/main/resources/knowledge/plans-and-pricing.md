# Free and Pro plans

**Free**: unlimited notes, sync across 2 devices, export single notes to Markdown or PDF.

**Pro**: sync across unlimited devices, bulk export of all notes as a ZIP, 90-day version history, offline notebooks, and priority support. Pro costs USD 6 per month or USD 60 per year.

You can upgrade at any time in **Settings → Plan**. When you upgrade in the middle of a billing period, you only pay the difference for the remaining days.
