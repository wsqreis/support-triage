# Reporting a bug

The fastest way to get a bug fixed is to send us diagnostic logs:

- **Desktop**: **Help → Send diagnostic logs**
- **Mobile**: **Settings → About → Send diagnostic logs**

Logs include your app version and device, but never the content of your notes. In your message, tell us what you expected, what happened instead, and whether it happens every time. If the app crashes on start, reinstalling is safe only after your notes have synced.
