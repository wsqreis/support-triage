# Deleting your account and data

Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Cancel any App Store or Google Play subscription first.

Your account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 14 days, including all notes, attachments and backups. During those 14 days you can cancel the deletion by signing in again.

We recommend exporting your notes first. You can also ask for a copy of all the personal data we hold about you by contacting support.
